# Finding Consensus from AI Alignment Studies: A Short Survey

This project surveys existing AI alignment studies to identify where different approaches agree and disagree about how to keep advanced AI systems aligned with human values, preferences, and goals, across two categories: human–AI and brain–AI representational alignment studies.

Since September 2024, I have not had a formal advisor; this literature survey has been conducted independently. This literature survey is also outside the NLP/NLU/neurosymbolic AI studies I have worked on previously.

I am grateful for the mentorship of my long‑term mentor, Prof. Tom Mitchell; his work on brain–AI alignment remains an inspiration for this survey.

I am grateful to the reviewers, organizers, and chairs of the CogInterp workshop (NeurIPS 2025) and the LM‑Reasoning Bridge Program (AAAI 2026) for their feedback and support.

**Note:** This work only targets to bring together a direct record of various research works/surveys on alignment and divergences within Human-AI / Brain-AI representational studies to the best of my knowledge. Therefore this does not include a position or any new discovery and hence novelty is not an intended goal/outcome.
